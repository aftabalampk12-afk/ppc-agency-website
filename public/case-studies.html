EXAMPLE RESULTS

From PPC Challenges To Measurable Improvement

These examples are representative scenarios for demonstrating our approach. Results vary by account, product, market, pricing and other business factors.

01AMAZON PPC

Consumer Accessories Brand

Client type: Established Amazon brand with multiple accessory SKUs and fragmented campaigns.

Challenge

Ad spend was spread across too many low-volume targets, search-term waste was high, and winning keywords were not receiving enough budget.

Strategy

  • Restructured campaigns by product, intent and match type.
  • Harvested converting search terms into dedicated exact-match campaigns.
  • Added negative keywords and product targets to reduce wasted spend.
  • Reallocated budget toward profitable products and high-converting terms.

Illustrative results

ROAS2.4x → 4.1x
ACOS41.7% → 24.4%
02WALMART PPC

Home & Outdoor Products Brand

Client type: Walmart marketplace seller with strong organic products but underdeveloped advertising.

Challenge

The account relied heavily on broad targeting, had inconsistent bid control and lacked a clear structure for scaling products with proven demand.

Strategy

  • Built dedicated campaigns around priority products and search themes.
  • Refined keyword targeting using performance and relevance signals.
  • Adjusted bids and budgets according to conversion and efficiency trends.
  • Separated discovery from scaling campaigns for cleaner optimization.

Illustrative results

ROAS2.1x → 3.6x
ACOS47.6% → 27.8%
03GOOGLE ADS

DTC E-commerce Brand

Client type: Direct-to-consumer brand looking to grow qualified traffic without sacrificing profitability.

Challenge

Shopping traffic was generating sales, but inefficient queries and weak budget allocation made it difficult to scale while maintaining target efficiency.

Strategy

  • Segmented products and search intent into clearer campaign groups.
  • Improved query control with exclusions and tighter targeting.
  • Prioritized high-margin products and stronger-converting traffic.
  • Used performance trends to guide bids, budgets and scaling decisions.

Illustrative results

ROAS2.7x → 4.5x
ACOS37.0% → 22.2%
